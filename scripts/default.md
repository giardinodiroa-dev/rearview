# ShadowTasker Default Cold-Call Script

> **Variables:** `{contact_name}` · `{company}` · `{agent_name}`

---

## Opening

Hi, may I speak with **{contact_name}**?

*(If gatekeeper: "This is {agent_name} calling — I have a quick message for {contact_name}.")*

Hi {contact_name}, this is **{agent_name}**. I'll keep this brief — do you have sixty seconds?

---

## Quick Pitch (30-Second Value Prop)

Great. We work with companies like {company} to help their sales teams spend less time on manual call logging and more time actually selling.

Most reps waste 20 to 30 minutes after every call updating the CRM, tagging dispositions, and writing follow-up notes. We automate all of that in real time — right inside whatever dialer or CRM you're already using — so your team captures better data without any extra steps.

I was hoping to find out if that's something that costs you time right now.

---

## Discovery Question

Can I ask — how does your team currently handle call logging and disposition tracking after each call?

*(Listen fully. Take notes. Do not interrupt.)*

---

## Objection: "Not Interested"

I hear you — and I'm not here to push anything. Most people I talk to say the same thing before they realize how much time their team is actually losing to it.

What I'd ask is just ten minutes on a call to show you exactly what it looks like in practice. If it doesn't fit, you'll know in ten minutes and I'll never bother you again.

Would Thursday or Friday morning work?

---

## Objection: "Send Me an Email"

Absolutely, happy to do that. Just so the email is actually useful to you — what's the one thing you'd want to know about it? Is it the integration side, the cost, or how it fits into your current workflow?

*(Let them answer, then:)*

Perfect. I'll tailor it exactly to that. I'll send it over today — and I'll give you a quick call Friday just to make sure it landed and answer any questions. Does late morning work?

---

## Close — Book a Meeting

Based on what you've shared, I think there's a real fit here. I'd love to set up a short 15-minute demo — no pitch deck, just a live walkthrough of how it works with your setup.

I have Tuesday at 10 AM or Wednesday at 2 PM open. Which works better for you?

*(Confirm time zone, email for calendar invite.)*

Perfect. I'll send a calendar invite to **[their email]** right now. Looking forward to connecting, {contact_name}.

---

## Voicemail Script

> *Leave when you reach voicemail. Keep it under 25 seconds.*

Hi {contact_name}, this is **{agent_name}** calling for {company}. I'll be quick — we help sales teams cut post-call admin time by automating CRM updates in real time. If that's something worth a ten-minute look, give me a call back at **[your number]** or I'll try you again Thursday morning. Thanks — have a great day.
